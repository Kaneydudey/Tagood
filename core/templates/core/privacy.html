Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Tagood Japanese is a small Japanese learning website created for students and members of the Tagood Japanese community. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your username and email address. We also store your learning progress, including exercise progress, confidence scores, and ranking points.

Your password is handled by Django’s built-in authentication system. We cannot see your actual password.

How we use your information

We use your information to let you log in, save your progress, show your profile stats, and display the global ranking page.

We may also use your email address to contact you about Tagood Japanese exercises, updates, or learning material.

Global ranking

If you use the learning exercises, your username and total confidence points may appear on the global ranking page. Your email address is not shown on the ranking page.

Support links

Our website may link to external support pages such as Patreon or Ko-fi. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your email address with other students.

Account deletion

If you want your account or learning data deleted, please contact Tagood Japanese directly.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact Tagood Japanese.
